## Context

Void Born is my own clothing brand. I started it because I love this aesthetic and want to explore that market. The site sells the brand's image first: the shirts are not in production yet.

## What I built

Lovable generated the first prototype, only the skeleton. I built everything after that myself, AI-assisted in Cursor: the frontend and the deploy.

- A catalog of nine shirts with a bag that lives in the browser (localStorage).
- The identity on screen: sigil linework behind the logo and Cinzel for the headings.
- React with Vite and Radix UI.

## Decisions

- **The brand before the store.** With no shirts in production, the site shows the identity and the catalog, and the bag stays in the browser instead of going to a checkout.

## Status

The site is live at void-born.vercel.app. The brand is paused, not closed.
