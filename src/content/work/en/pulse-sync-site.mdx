## Context

Pulse Sync is where I work as a self-employed automation developer, since August 2025. This is its website.

I built the first version in React in July 2025. This one is the second, from May 2026, and it uses no framework.

## What I built

I designed the site and built it from scratch with AI-assisted development: the frontend and the deploy.

- A particle orb in the hero: 5,200 points on a Fibonacci sphere (2,400 on mobile), displaced by simplex noise in a custom shader, paused when the tab is hidden.
- "What happens inside an automation": a 5-step walkthrough with three scenarios (AI chatbot, scheduling, lead management) that explains an n8n flow to someone who has never seen one.
- A chat widget for visitors' questions.

## Decisions

- **A tech, futuristic look.** Pulse Sync sells automation, so the site should look like technology.

## What broke

The two parts that broke while I was building this version were the three.js sphere and the chat widget.
